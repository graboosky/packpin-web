# PackPin Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-09

PackPin is an iPhone app that shows Pokémon TCG vending machines near you and keeps a
private log of your visits. This policy describes everything the app does with
information. It is short because the app does little.

## What PackPin collects

Nothing. PackPin has no account, no sign-in, and no analytics. It sends nothing about you
anywhere. Your visits and saved machines stay on this phone.

## Location

When you tap the locate control, PackPin asks for permission to use your location while the
app is open, and uses it only to sort machines by distance from you. Your location is never
stored and never leaves your phone. PackPin never asks for always-on location.

## Your visits and saved machines

The visits you log and the machines you save are stored on your phone only. PackPin does
not upload, sync, or back them up. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Purchases

Weekly and lifetime access are sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes
the payment and holds the receipt; PackPin learns only whether your access is active. Apple's
handling of purchase data is described in [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## The machine list

The list of machines ships inside the app and is updated with the app. Browsing it makes no
request to any server.

## Maps

Machines are shown on Apple Maps. Loading a map is a request to Apple's servers under Apple's
privacy policy; PackPin adds nothing of yours to it.

## Children

PackPin collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

When this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and the new text is published at
this address before the app version it describes ships.
