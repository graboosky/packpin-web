# PackPin

PackPin is an iPhone app for Pokémon TCG collectors who walk or drive to a vending machine
and are tired of finding it empty.

- Every Pokémon TCG vending machine we know of, official and independent, on one map.
- Stocked, sold out, or gone. One tap after every visit, kept on your phone.
- After a few visits, PackPin shows which weekday that machine has been full.
