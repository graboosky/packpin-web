# PackPin Support

Write to **p.grabowski.kontakt@gmail.com**. Replies usually come within a few days.

## What to include

- Your iPhone model and iOS version
- The PackPin version, shown at the bottom of About
- What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead

## Suggest a machine

PackPin's machine list is kept by hand. If you know a machine that is missing or wrong, send
these three lines — the same ones the app offers under About → Suggest a machine:

```
Where is it? (city, venue, level)
Who runs it?
What does it sell?
```

## Purchases and refunds

Weekly and lifetime access are sold through Apple. To manage or cancel the weekly
subscription, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Refunds are handled
by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).
